- [2026-07-14] LOB flow as one-sided directional veto, not symmetric confirmation gate. Live incident: day_scanner picked up BWET (top gainer) after engine restart; momentum=1.0/1.0 maxed the ensemble to 0.558 and triggered a long entry. LOB flow was −0.24 (score) / 0.43 (confidence) — clear selling pressure — but its −0.04 contribution was swamped by the +0.60 momentum term. Position entered at $270, immediately reversed, unrealised loss −$259 with no stop (separate fill-timing bug fixed same session). Two design questions surfaced and answered with data (500 signals, 4 trades): (1) Gate threshold: gate=0.0 (block any negative LOB) would have blocked JPM at −0.01, a +$16.78 winner. LOB scores among buy-candidates (ensemble ≥ 0.40) average −0.054 — the distribution is centred slightly negative, so gate=0.0 is almost a coin flip. Only 3% of all signals go below −0.20; that is where real adverse flow lives. Chosen: block when score < −0.10 AND confidence ≥ 0.30. (2) Symmetric gate (require positive LOB to buy): rejected. 73% of buy-candidates have negative LOB; requiring score > 0 blocks 73% of opportunities, and requiring score > +0.10 with conf ≥ 0.30 blocks 100% — the engine would never trade. LOB flow is a lagging microstructure signal; by the time it turns positive, market makers have already repriced and the opportunity has passed. Correct role: one-sided veto on strong confident opposing flow, not two-sided confirmation. Parameters: lob_flow_gate=0.10, lob_flow_min_conf=0.30, applied at new-entry only (exits/adjustments are never blocked by this gate). Also fixed concurrently: ATR stop was staged after om.submit() but applied to state.positions before the async fill event populated it; stop was None on every new position. Fix: _pending_stops dict staged at submit, applied in _on_fill_event after on_fill() guarantees the position exists.
- [2026-07-14] Trailing ATR stop for day trades. Live incident: NXTC ran +5.7% from $6.48 to $6.85 but the engine held through the reversal and closed at a loss because the initial ATR stop was set once at entry and never moved. Fixed by advancing the stop on every 5-min rebalance cycle: for each open long position, compute trailing = price − stop_atr_multiple × ATR(14, 5m) and update stop_price only if trailing > current stop (ratchet — never retreats). Applies to: (1) intraday positions where stop_price is already set (pending_stops gap at new entries prevents premature overwrite); (2) xsec addons where stop_price is initialised here (retag cleared it on the base). check_stops() amended: for xsec positions it closes to pos.xsec_qty (addon only) and immediately clears stop_price so the stop doesn't re-fire on the next bar while the exit order is pending. With trailing stops: a +5.7% run that reverses would have been exited at ~price − mult×ATR instead of waiting for signal flip or EOD, locking in most of the gain.

- [2026-07-14] Intraday add-ons on xsec holdings. Observation: all 10 xsec holdings (CIEN, SNDK, LITE, WDC, ECHO, TER, INTC, STX, COHR, MU) were up on the same session as the NXTC discussion, averaging +$271 unrealised each. Root cause: 12-1 momentum selects stocks under institutional accumulation; that buying happens throughout each trading day, creating persistent intraday LOB imbalance in the same direction. The 5-min ensemble signal on these names fires the same thesis at a shorter timescale. Decision: allow the intraday book to place long-only add-on positions on top of xsec holdings when the ensemble confirms. These are EOD-flattened each day; the monthly base stays. Never allow intraday shorts on xsec names (would fight the book). Architecture: (1) Position.xsec_qty tracks shares owned by the xsec book; intraday_addon = pos.qty − pos.xsec_qty. (2) retag() initialises xsec_qty = pos.qty on first call; syncs holdings[sym] = xsec_qty (not pos.qty) so rebalance deltas are computed against the correct baseline even when an addon exists. (3) Risk manager: relaxed cross-book guard — intraday long on xsec long passes; intraday short on xsec still rejects. Addon size checked against tier.max_position_pct × equity (delta only, not total). (4) _xsec_intraday_coids set in engine tags all three COID variants (entry, -rp, -mkt) so the fill handler routes correctly. (5) EOD flattener closes pos.qty − pos.xsec_qty per xsec symbol; signal-flip exit also closes addon back to xsec_qty. (6) xsec holdings warmed up and added to _live_universe at startup so the stream delivers bars and signals are computed. Also: 10:30am ET time gate in _enter_or_adjust for screener picks (not base-universe, not xsec) — volume on small/mid-caps collapses after the opening hour; xsec symbols are liquid all session and exempt.

- [2026-07-14] Screener quality filters and midday rescan to replicate NXTC-class trades. Live observation: NXTC (screener pick, +$95 unrealised, +5.7%) outperformed all fixed-universe intraday trades. Root cause of under-performance on base universe: mega-caps (SPY, AAPL, NVDA) are priced to nanoseconds by HFT; 5-min free IEX data has no edge there. Screener picks have real catalyst momentum and real LOB imbalance. Three improvements: (1) Volume filter — require min $5M dollar volume at scan time (price × cumulative volume). BWET ($270 loss) had thin float and noisy LOB; this filter would have excluded it. (2) Pct-change floor — require ≥3% up on the day for gainers list, filtering drift from real catalyst moves. (3) Composite scoring — rank candidates by √(pct_change × dollar_volume) rather than raw list order. This rewards stocks with both momentum AND liquidity, not just one. (4) Midday rescan — fire the scanner again 2h after open (~11:30am ET) to catch breakouts that consolidate then resume, which is a different and often stronger setup than the opening gap play. Slots: open+60s (gap plays), open+7200s (midday breakouts). Each slot only adds NEW symbols; existing live-universe symbols never removed (open positions need bars). WebSocket budget consumed in order; if 30-slot cap is hit the slot is skipped.
